Purpose Employee of the Quarter Award is to publicly recognize outstanding employees and acknowledge their accomplishments and contributions to the company generally.
It also offers an opportunity for all employees within the Company to recognize their peers for their exceptional job performance.
Nomination Criteria The primary criteria for consideration is service and job performance that goes beyond the daily work of an employee.
Listed below are the proposed criteria for which Nexcel employees may be nominated.
Achievements Nominee exhibits high quality in completing tasks and goes above and beyond assigned duties.
Work is consistently thorough and accurate over the latest 3-month period of time.
Reliability A reliable employee is one who shows up for work on time and is prepared to complete his work in a timely manner.
His colleagues and his leader can always trust that he/she will perform the activity he/she was assigned.
Creativity Nominee offers new and innovative ideas resulting in the improvement of efficiency and/or the effectiveness of a process or procedure.
Learning & Development Nominee's interest in developing themselves, his/her interest in being part of trainings and in improving the knowledge.
Attitude/Teamwork Works to foster collaboration, communication, and cooperation among peers.
Nominee is cooperative, helpful and consistently displays a positive attitude while interacting with others.
Nomination Process - All full time employees, who have completed his/her initial probationary period are eligible to be nominated except Management staff, Selection Committee.
- There will be few Awards for qualifying employees nominated.
- Each nominator is requested to nominate one nominee. Self nominations are NOT accepted.
- Former Employees of the (previous) Years and former Employees of the (previous) Quarters are also eligible for this nomination.
- Nominators may nominate the other nominator or the employee from other team/department.
- Nominators may be contacted for additional information.
