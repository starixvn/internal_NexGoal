Purpose Employee recognition is an integral part of an organization.
The purpose of this employee appreciation program is to recognize outstanding contributions of employee in that Year.
Nomination Criteria The primary criteria for consideration is service and job performance that goes beyond the daily work of an employee.
Listed below are 5 proposed criteria for which Nexcel employees may be nominated.
To nominate an employee, please complete the information requested on sheet "II. Nomination"
Professional achievements Exemplary performance in the employee’s own responsibilities as well as service beyond assigned duties.
Established record of significant achievement sustained over the 1-year period of time.
Leadership Setting a good example or being a positive role model for other employees.
Describe how this nominee demonstrates high integrity, exceptional work habits and strong character that influence his or her fellow employees, and give specific examples.
Innovation/Creativity Presenting new ideas and helpful suggestions.
Describe creative ideas and suggestions that this nominee has contributed that have helped improve productivity, workflow, efficiency or enhanced the overall success of the company.
Include unique or innovative product designs/processes or ideas that helped improve quality.
Contributions at Company/Department level List out the contributions that had a significant influence on the department/company if any.
Teamwork/Personality Describe how this employee consistently assists and works with fellow employees without having to be asked; eagerly participates in group or team projects; and thinks of others and the needs of the company before thinking of self. Give specific examples.
Nomination Process - All full time employees, who have completed the 1-year service as at start date of the nomination are eligible to be nominated except Management staff, Selection Committee.
- There will be few Awards for qualifying employees nominated.
- Self nominations are accepted and recognized. It means each nominator can nominate him/herself together with up to other 2 nominees.
- Former Employees of the (previous) Years and former Employees of the (previous) Quarters are also eligible for this nomination.
- Nominators may nominate the other nominator or the employee from other team/department.
- Nominators may be contacted for additional information.
