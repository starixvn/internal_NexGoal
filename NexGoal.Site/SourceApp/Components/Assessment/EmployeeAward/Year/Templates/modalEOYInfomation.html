Purpose Employee recognition is an integral part of an organization. The purpose of this appreciation program is to recognize employees who have made significant contributions to Nexcel in [year].
Nomination Criteria The primary criteria for consideration are job performance and contributing quality of the employee. Listed below are 5 proposed criteria for which Nexcel employees may be nominated.
Professional achievements Exemplary performance and service beyond employee responsibilities. Detail his/her established records of great achievement sustained during the [year].
Leadership Describe how this nominee demonstrates high integrity, exceptional work habits and strong character for being a positive role model for other employees, and give specific examples.
Innovation/Creativity Describe his/her creative ideas and helpful suggestions that have helped improve the productivity and enhanced the overall success of the company.
Contributions at Company/Department level List out the significant contributions to the department/company if any.
Teamwork/Personality Describe how this employee voluntarily assists and works with coworkers; eagerly participates in groups or projects; and focus on the needs of others instead of his/her needs. Give specific examples.
Process - Nominee must be a full time staff and has been employed at Nexcel for at least 1 year as at 30th November [this year].
- Management staff and Selection Committee are not eligible for this award.
- Nominator can vote for up-to 3 nominees. Nominee can be the employee of other team/department.
- Self-nomination is accepted.
- The Selection Committee will determine the final award recipients.
