Purpose Employee recognition is an integral part of an organization. The purpose of this appreciation program is to recognize employees who have made significant contributions to Nexcel in the last quarter.
Nomination Criteria The primary criteria for consideration are job performance and contributing quality of the employee. Listed below are 5 proposed criteria for which Nexcel employees may be nominated.
Achievements Exemplary performance and service beyond employee responsibilities. Detail his/her established records of great achievement sustained during the last quarter.
Reliability His/her ability to handle tasks properly and work under pressure with minimal supervision.
Innovation/Creativity Describe his/her creative ideas and helpful suggestions that have helped improve the productivity and enhanced the overall success of the company.
Learning & Development His/her dedication to learn new knowledge and improve themselves, give specific examples.
Teamwork/Personality Describe how this employee voluntarily assists and works with coworkers; eagerly participates in groups or projects; and focus on the needs of others instead of his/her needs. Give specific examples.
Process - Nominee must be a full time staff and has completed his/her probationary period.
- Management staff and Selection Committee are not eligible for this award.
- Nominator can vote for only 1 nominee. Nominee can be the employee of other team/department.
- Self-nomination is NOT accepted.
- The Selection Committee will determine the final award recipients.
